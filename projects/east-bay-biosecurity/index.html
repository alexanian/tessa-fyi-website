East Bay Biosecurity
2021 2020 Summit 2018-2019 Team

From 2018 to 2021, East Bay Biosecurity brought together early-career professionals, graduate students, and other curious researchers to explore biosecurity and pandemic preparedness.

The group hosted regular events at The Berkeley REACH from 2018-2019, using a Center for Effective Altruism Community-Building Grant to send organizers to biosecurity-related conferences. In 2020, the organizing team took a break to focus on Catalyst Biosummit, and then the COVID-19 pandemic made it unwise to host an in-person meetup group. In 2021, a new organizing team hosted virtual meetups for several months.

Although the group is no longer active, if you are interested in connecting with our network or getting advice about hosting your own biosecurity-focused events, feel free to reach out to hello@tessa.fyi.
