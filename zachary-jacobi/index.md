Zachary Jacobi was my favourite person in the world from sometime in early 2015 until the day they died. Remembering how excellent Zach was is a very poor substitute for having them in my life, but it’s the best I’ve got.

![Photograph of Zachary Jacobi and I, wearing matching outfits. I am kissing Zach's cheek and they are grinning.](/img/zachary-jacobi/space-wedding-kiss.png){: .img-fluid}

This is my favourite photo of the two of us. I wish we'd taken more photos together.
{: .caption}

## Writing about Zach

[Eulogy](/zachary-jacobi/eulogy)<br />
what I said at Zach's funeral on August 16, 2019.

> I hope that each of you, at some point in your life, gets an opportunity to experience something like what Zach and I had.

[A year without you](/zachary-jacobi/a-year-without-you)<br />
what I wrote on August 7, 2020, the first anniversary of Zach’s death.

> I love you. I wish I had more to show for it. I wish there were more of you left in the world to love.

[In the future there will be no one like you](/zachary-jacobi/in-the-future-no-one-like-you)<br />
an essay I started before Zach died, with a title that meant something different by the time I finished it on August 7, 2022.

> If I examined the worry underneath all my more flattering concerns, I suspect I’d have found this: what if there aren’t people like you in the future?

## Zach's writing

Zach wrote over a million words in the time they were alive, many of them on their blog, [Socratic Form Microscopy](https://socratic-form-microscopy.com/). In retrospect, it’s kind of ridiculous how many ideas they were generating every week.

You can find a few other traces of Zach on the internet, if you’re curious. They wrote around [100 reviews](https://www.goodreads.com/review/list/46626554-zachary-jacobi?sort=review&view=reviews) and collected some [quotes](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/list/46626554) on Goodreads, though the [Book Review tag](https://tessa.fyi/socratic-form-microscopy/tag/book-review) on their blog are a much more substantial picture of what they were reading. Zach had taken the [Giving What We Can](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/) pledge, and there is an [Against Malaria Foundation](https://www.againstmalaria.com/Fundraiser.aspx?FundraiserID=8259) fundraiser in their name.
